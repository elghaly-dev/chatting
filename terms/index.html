Terms of Service

These terms apply to Chatting, a paid service from elghaly at chatting.elghaly.dev.

The service

Chatting offers private, one-to-one online sessions. We help you choose, set up and train an AI assistant on your own accounts, with you at the keyboard. We guide; you stay in control of every login and click.

Courses

  • Starter: three 1:1 sessions within 35 days — safety basics, choosing an assistant, and first daily tasks.
  • Full: ten 1:1 sessions within 35 days — everything in Starter, plus deeper training, permissions, tool connections, and daily routines.
  • Flexible: 1:1 sessions when you need them, inside 35 days or beyond, on the topics you pick each time.

Every session is private and priced per client. You receive a fixed price by email before anything is booked.

Booking and rescheduling

Request a quote, confirm a course, and schedule or reschedule sessions by email. Nothing is booked until you agree to the price and times we propose.

What we never ask for

We never ask for or accept your passwords, keys, seed phrases, or card details — not by email, not on a call, not in a session. If anyone asks in our name, it is not us.

Your results

Outcomes depend on how you use your own tools and accounts after our sessions. We teach and set up with you; we make no promise of specific results. Check your assistant’s work before you rely on it.

Refunds

If a session was not useful, ask and you get your money back: no form and no time limit. Email support@elghaly.dev. See the refund policy and our privacy policy.

Итог зависит от того, как вы сами используете инструменты и аккаунты после сессий. Мы обучаем и настраиваем вместе с вами; мы не обещаем конкретных результатов. Проверяйте работу ассистента, прежде чем на неё опираться.
