Privacy Policy

This policy describes Chatting at chatting.elghaly.dev.

We deeply respect your privacy rights under the CCPA/CPRA. We collect the minimum (just your email and what kind of work you do), never sell or share your personal information, and you can ask us to delete it anytime at support@elghaly.dev.

What we collect

Your email address and a short description of the kind of work you do when you request a quote or book sessions. We do not ask for your name, company name, passwords, keys, or payment details by email.

Why we use it

To reply with a quote, schedule sessions, and support you during your course. We use it for Chatting and related email from elghaly, and for nothing else.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed for those purposes, and no longer. You can ask us to delete it at any time; we will remove it when you ask.

No selling or sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Cookies and analytics on this site

The Chatting site (including this page and the home page) loads no third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking scripts. We serve fonts from our own domain. We do not set marketing cookies on these pages.

Deletion requests

Email support@elghaly.dev from the address you used with us and ask us to delete your information. We will confirm when it is done.

Children

Chatting is for adults who can enter into a paid service agreement. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of the page shows when it last changed. Continued use of Chatting after an update means you accept the revised policy.
